Maestro

Your piano coach. It listens, grades you, and takes you from first notes to mastery.

Put your iPad on the music stand in landscape, then tap Start.

Let's set up listening

  1. MicrophoneAllow access so Maestro can hear your piano.
  2. Silence checkStay quiet for two seconds while I measure the room.
  3. Play middle CPlay the C near the middle of your keyboard.

Tip: the iPad microphone is at the top edge. On the music stand it hears the piano well. Digital piano with MIDI? Plug it in and Maestro will use it too (in browsers that support Web MIDI).
